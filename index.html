How to play

Grab & throw
Drag a sculpture upward to lift and stretch it, then release to throw. Tap it for a bounce. Grab a stroke off-center to make the sculpture swing. Its name also works as a handle.
Stack
Drag a logo over another until its name lights up, then release. On a touchscreen, one finger lifts and carries the jelly. Both pieces wobble on contact, and the lower one keeps its dent until you lift the weight.
Softness
Turn the rim clockwise for softer jelly, or drag the center upward. A corner grip lets the rest sag under its weight. Drop it on an edge to send a ripple through it. The arrow keys fine-tune the dial.
Turn & orbit
Choose Turn and drag a sculpture to see its sides and back. Drag the empty floor to orbit the whole studio.
Light
Slide the light angle to sweep the colored caustics across the floor. The range also responds to arrow keys.
Forces
Hold Attract or Repel over the floor to move nearby pieces. Release the force and gravity carries the sculptures back to the floor.
Keyboard
Tab to a sculpture. Enter bounces it. The arrows throw it, or rotate it with Turn selected. S shakes the studio.
